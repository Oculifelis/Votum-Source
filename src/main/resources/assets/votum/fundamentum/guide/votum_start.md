## Wax

- Set a **Seal Brazier** over a lit campfire, fire, magma or lava.
- Add 4 honeycomb and 1 pigment. The pigment picks the wax.
- After 10 seconds the brazier holds 4 **Sealing Wax**. Use it with an empty hand to take it.

{{item votum:seal_brazier votum:sealing_wax}}

## Sealing a warrant

- Place a **Blank Warrant** on a **Signet Desk**, then add the pact's wax.
- Use the matching **Signet** on the desk. The signet and wax together name the pact.
- Take the **Sealed Warrant** and use it to swear the pact.

{{item votum:blank_warrant votum:signet_desk votum:sealed_warrant}}

No more than 3 pacts can be sworn at once. Each pact is sworn only once.
