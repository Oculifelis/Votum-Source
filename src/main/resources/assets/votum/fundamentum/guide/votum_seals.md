A fulfilled pact turns its warrant into a **Fulfilled Warrant**. Put it on a Signet Desk with a **Signet Ring** or **Seal Pendant**, whichever the pact names, and use the signet to press it.

- Pressed pieces go in the two **Seal** slots and give the pact's boon while worn.
- A cracked piece gives nothing until it is restamped at the desk with 2 of its wax.

## Charter Post

The owner pins a sealed warrant to a **Charter Post**. Anyone can copy it onto a Blank Warrant with 1 matching wax. Empty-hand use lists sworn and fulfilled pacts. A comparator reads how many pacts the owner has fulfilled.

## Packs

Pacts, waxes and the Village and Illager Host factions are datapack JSON. Packs can add pacts, change oaths, clauses and boons, or add new waxes and pigments.
