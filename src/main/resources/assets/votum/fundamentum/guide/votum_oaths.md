Every pact has an **oath** to finish and a **clause** to keep.

- An oath asks for kills, breeding, trades, or days without killing a certain creature.
- A clause forbids one act, such as hurting a villager or dying to the undead.
- Breaking a clause voids an unfinished pact. A finished pact's seal piece cracks instead, and its faction standing drops by 50.

Progress shows above the hotbar as it grows, and any Charter Post lists every sworn pact.

## The six pacts

Each pact is named by its signet and wax. Grave Watch takes the Skull Signet and Bone Wax. Shepherd's Compact takes the Hoof Signet and Verdant Wax. Merchant's Bond takes the Coin Signet and Gilded Wax. Silk Truce takes the Fang Signet and Ink Wax. Drowned Accord takes the Tide Signet and Tidal Wax. Ender Covenant takes the Eye Signet and Crimson Wax.
